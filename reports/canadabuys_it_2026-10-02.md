**Technical requirements (extracted):**
  - The CITT, supported by the ATSSC, is seeking information from vendors regarding commercially available solutions that could support the administration of questionnaires used by the Trade Remedies Investigations Branch (TRIB).
  - The process involves collecting information from Canadian and international stakeholders and requires the secure handling of sensitive information (Protected B) throughout its lifecycle.
  - Protected B is the federal level of security that applies to information and assets that, if compromised, could cause serious injury to an individual, organization, or government.
  - The purpose of this Request for Information (RFI) is to gather information on available solutions that can provide a secure, web-based platform for questionnaire management and respondent engagement.
  - The ATSSC is interested in understanding the capabilities, functionality, implementation considerations, security controls, and hosting models available in the marketplace.
  - The desired Online Platform would support the design and management of complex questionnaires, secure respondent access, data collection and storage, reporting and analytics, workflow automation, and e-mail management.
  - The Online Platform must support both internal and external users and provide functionality that promotes efficiency, usability, accessibility, and data integrity.
  - Given the sensitive nature of the information collected by the CITT, the solution must be capable of meeting Government of Canada security requirements, including the protection of Protected B information, data residency within Canada, robust access controls, audit logging, and compliance with applicable Government of Canada cloud and security standards.
