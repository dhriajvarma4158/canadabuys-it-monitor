  • The mandatory technical criteria are:
  • Liquid Chromatography (LC) System
  • The LC system must be supplied with an LC solution suitable for proteomics, such as a nano-flow LC system for peptide sample preparation and separation, designed for seamless coupling to the MS.
  • The system must operate at flow rates ranging from at least 200 nL/min to 100 µL/min and must not require a flow splitter.
  • The LC system must have the maximum operating pressure at least to 15,000 psi.
  • The LC system must be capable of operating within a pH range of 2–10.
  • The LC system must support two-dimensional (2D) LC analysis, including an auxiliary solvent and trap valve manager, a column-trapping pump, nano-Lock spray capability, primary check valves, column compartment, and switching valves configured for 2D operation.
  • The LC system must support unattended operation, with full diagnostic data display for up to 96 hours through the control software.
  • The LC system must include solvent storage, with a solvent tray capable of accommodating up to three (3) eluents.
  • The LC system must include a micro-binary solvent delivery system, supporting solvents A1 or A2 and B1 or B2, and must provide solvent conditioning, gradient formation, primary check valves, compressibility compensation, and automatic priming.
  • The system must be equipped with an integrated, programmable active wash system to flush the rear of the high-pressure seals.
